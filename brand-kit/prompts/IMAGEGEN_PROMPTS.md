# Prompty źródłowe — Driftwatch „Quiet Signal”

Cztery opisane poniżej tła zostały wygenerowane w trybie wbudowanego narzędzia
`imagegen`. Nie używano fallbacku CLI ani zewnętrznego klucza API. Obrazy nie
zawierają tekstu, dzięki czemu mogą działać w polskiej i angielskiej wersji
produktu.

## 1. Hero desktop

Plik źródłowy: `../backgrounds/sources/hero-quiet-signal.png`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: premium SaaS landing-page hero background for Driftwatch, a B2B web-change monitoring product
Primary request: create an original, cinematic editorial image expressing “Quiet Signal — meaningful change emerging from noise.” A deep charcoal observation field is built from layered translucent browser-page planes, fine horizontal baselines, and subtle paper/glass textures. One precise acid-lime signal gently departs from a baseline in a single clean notch, then returns; a restrained teal glint and one tiny warm coral reflection may appear as secondary accents.
Scene/backdrop: dark studio environment, near-black green charcoal, architectural depth, no literal room
Style/medium: high-end photorealistic macro product photography blended with refined 3D material rendering; tactile dark glass, smoked acrylic, coated paper; premium European B2B SaaS art direction, calm and credible
Composition/framing: wide 16:9 landscape; generous clean negative space across the left 48% for white headline copy; visual concentration on the right and lower-right; safe central crop for responsive screens; no important detail at the edges
Lighting/mood: quiet, controlled, low-key studio lighting, subtle volumetric depth, confident and reassuring rather than dramatic
Color palette: #101510 and #151a15, acid-lime #b4e653 as the single signal, restrained teal #36b8ad, extremely small coral #ff6b4a accent
Materials/textures: smoked glass, matte coated paper, fine grain, precise thin luminous line; sophisticated but not glossy
Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no watermark, no people, no devices, no literal dashboard, no charts with labels; keep contrast low enough behind left-side copy; the signal line must remain simple and readable
Avoid: neon cyberpunk, generic AI network imagery, glowing globes, robots, circuit boards, noisy particle clouds, stock-photo look, excessive gradients, multiple competing bright lines
```
